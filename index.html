    Translation

    Translation runs on your device. Chrome's built-in translator is used when your browser has it; otherwise a translation model downloads once and then works offline.

    AI reading

    Recipe Tidy reads recipes with built-in rules for free. For very messy files, scanned PDFs and photos, you can let Claude read them instead using your own Anthropic API key.

    Your key stays in this browser only and is sent straight to Anthropic. Get one at console.anthropic.com.
